# Privacy Policy — Robotics Tool

Last updated: 2026-09-25

Robotics Tool does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

## Data We Do Not Collect
- Personal information
- Location data
- Usage analytics
- Any data transmitted through ROS2 topics

## Local Storage
The app stores only, locally on the device:
- The last entered rosbridge address (IP/host and port)
- Up to 5 recently connected rosbridge addresses, which you can remove in the app
- Theme preference

Nothing is sent anywhere except to the rosbridge server you connect to.
